| R1 | **The demo now depends on three running parts.** On Day 1 the gap was "not wired." Now it is wired, and the quote list needs the API, the database and the dev proxy all running. If any one is down, the list fails to load. | Delivery Lead | M | H | Check all three before every demo: the ports, the health route, and the quote count. | R2 | **The premium is calculated in two places.** The client estimates on screen; the API computes the premium of record. On Day 2 they disagreed, $425 against $450, until the client's values were aligned. The duplication remains. | Front-end and platform teams; Delivery Lead (tracking) | M | H | Aligned as configuration, with the server as the source of truth. Explained in the decision memo. | R3 | **Leftover processes look like bugs.** Port 3000 can be held by the API dev server, the container, or a cluster port-forward. On Day 2, servers from the previous session were still running, so a new client started on port 5174 and the browser would have shown old code. | Delivery Lead | H | M | Before starting any server, check which ports are in use. Stop leftovers with `fuser -k`, restart cleanly, and confirm that a fresh startup line appears. | Any result that contradicts the code on disk, or a server choosing an unexpected port. |
